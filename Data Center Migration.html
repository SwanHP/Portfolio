DECISION 01

Reused the existing Active Directory forest instead of building new. Lower risk, faster path, no re-provisioning of 510 identities.

DECISION 02

Cleaned the entire organization’s accounts and group policies first, before any migration work, so the merge had nothing stale to carry over.

DECISION 03

Migrated users in groups rather than all at once, containing the blast radius of any single group’s issue.

RISK REGISTER, KEY ITEMS
MITIGATE

Unstable client-side internet and a narrow channel for database transfer. Response: minimize exposure, stand up a backup channel for the migration window.

TRANSFER

Loss of working resources mid-project (materialized). Response: transfer, a standing contact base of outsource specialists ready to slot in.

ACCEPT→ AVOID

Legacy systems and unknown migration complexity. Initial response: accept (drove the full 154-day timeline). Revised after the Phase 1 audit surfaced the legacy footprint early: avoid, retire the legacy systems and replace them with modern equivalents instead of migrating them as-is.
