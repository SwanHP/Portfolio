ASSUMPTIONS & CONSTRAINTS

  • A1Tool had to hold a high load, roughly 800–1,500 concurrent users with video streaming enabled.
  • A2Needed enough diversification to support many simultaneous classrooms, and retain context and learning materials after disconnect.
  • A3Had to be simple to pick up. Institutions had no time to train teaching staff.

TEAM (1)

  • PMIndustry-solution specialization and no-code MS knowledge, enough to run and close the project without an additional team.
RISK & OPPORTUNITY REGISTER
TRANSFER

Near-perfect fit to client requirements, but Teams is a complex tool to learn from zero. Response: roll out functionality gradually and hand off training responsibility to the institution’s IT department.

MITIGATE

Tool required further development. Response: build it out with Power Automate and Teams’ own customization capabilities.

EXPLOIT

Near-perfect fit to requirements already present in MS Teams itself. Response: build an MVP to maximize the demonstration of the tool’s strengths.
