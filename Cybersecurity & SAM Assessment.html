  • SAM LEADLicensing methodology and compliance calculation.
  • TECH SPECIALISTInventory tooling and vulnerability scanning.
KEY FINDINGS
TECHNICAL / CYBERSECURITY

Unsupported legacy software still in production, unpatched browsers, cross-entity resource sharing without formal affiliation, and no controls over what gets installed on end-user machines.

ORGANIZATIONAL

No formalized software procurement or accounting process, no install controls or access segmentation, and purchasing not aligned to the legal entity actually using the software.

LICENSING

License shortfalls in some legal entities and idle, unused licenses in others, at the same time, plus usage-rights violations across the license estate.

METRICS TRACKED

CIS IG scoring

Implementation Group level (0–3) per competency, 20 competencies total.

License compliance ratio

Owned licenses vs. required, calculated per software category and per legal entity.

5-year TCO comparison

On-premise licensing vs. cloud subscription, modeled per category.

Outcome. Assessment across 20 CIS competencies surfaced the weakest maturity in incident response and software-supply security. Licensing analysis found simultaneous shortfalls and idle surplus across the client's legal entities. The 5-year TCO model showed a potential reduction of roughly 40% in licensing spend by moving core office and infrastructure workloads to a cloud subscription model.
